## How to sign

Check the "Contributor License Agreement" box in your pull request. The
PR record — your GitHub account, the timestamp, the diff — is our copy of
this agreement.

Prefer not to sign? That's completely reasonable; please open an issue
describing the fix instead. A well-described bug is genuinely valuable
and needs no agreement.
